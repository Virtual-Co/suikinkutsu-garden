About the science

Suikinkutsu are garden features where drops of water fall into a buried vessel and create a soft, resonant sound. Haimes, Uhde, and Marutschke describe a way to characterize recordings using six acoustic descriptors. This toy adds a synthesized sound and a note-matching game; its pot controls and target notes are authored for play and are not measurements from the paper’s nine sites. It does not recreate their analysis or physical installation. Read the paper: “A computational framework for the acoustic characterization of Suikinkutsu (water harp cave)” (Paul Haimes, Alarith Uhde, Daniel Moritz Marutschke).

Sound is synthesized locally after your first tap. No recordings or network requests.

All controls work with mouse, touch, and keyboard.
